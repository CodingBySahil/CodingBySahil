## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hello! I'm **Muhammad Sahil**, widely known online as **CodingBySahil**. I'm a passionate **Full Stack MERN Developer**, **Computer Science student**, **AI Instructor**, and lifelong learner who enjoys transforming ideas into modern, scalable, and impactful software solutions.

My journey into programming started with curiosity and gradually evolved into a deep passion for software engineering. Today, I spend most of my time designing beautiful user interfaces, developing robust backend systems, building complete full-stack applications, exploring Artificial Intelligence, mentoring aspiring developers, and continuously learning emerging technologies.

I believe software development is far more than writing code—it's about solving meaningful problems, creating exceptional user experiences, and building products that make people's lives easier. Every project I build is an opportunity to improve my technical expertise while maintaining high standards of quality, performance, scalability, maintainability, accessibility, and clean architecture.

I enjoy working across the complete software development lifecycle—from brainstorming ideas and planning architectures to designing interfaces, implementing business logic, optimizing databases, deploying applications, and continuously improving products based on user feedback.

# 💼 Professional Experience

My professional journey has been driven by a passion for building high-quality software and sharing knowledge with others. Through both industry experience and teaching, I have developed a well-rounded skill set that combines technical expertise with effective communication.
